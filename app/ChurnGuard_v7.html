How the prediction works
Step 1 — Standardise: scaled = (value − mean) / std
Step 2 — Log-odds: z = −0.6723 + Σ(coef_i × scaled_i)
Step 3 — Sigmoid: P(churn) = 1 / (1 + e^(−z))
Step 4 — Classify: P≥70% → Critical · 50–70% → High · 30–50% → Moderate · <30% → Low
